Before you vote

Four things every voter should know about how a Rollcall election works.

You vote once

Your email is checked against the roll. After you submit your ballot, the code cannot be used again.

Voting has a window

Ballots are accepted only between the start and end time set by the organiser. Outside that window, the form will not open.

Results come after

No running totals are shown while voting is open. The count is published once the window closes.

  • Not on the roll? Contact the election organiser to be added.
  • Wrong email or ID? The form will tell you which one did not match.
  • Final choice: A submitted vote cannot be changed or withdrawn.
